    FAQ

    Good questions.

    How reliable is FlyyAir suspension?

    Cutting corners on suspension always comes back to bite you. With quality components, proper installation and basic care, a FlyyAir system can easily outlast the life of your vehicle.

    What maintenance does my FlyyAir system need?

    Not much. Empty your air tank every couple of months, and if you have water traps, drain them at least once a month. Both drain moisture from the system, and both matter less in dry climates. An easy habit: drain the traps while you fill up with gas.
